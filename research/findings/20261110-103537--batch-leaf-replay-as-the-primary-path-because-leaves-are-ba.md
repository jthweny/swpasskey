## FOUND
- Yubico's own FIDO2 metadata for U2F-family models carries a populated **`attestationCertificateKeyIdentifiers`** list — five SHA-1 identifiers under `description: "YubiKey 5Ci"`, `protocolFamily: "u2f"` — so the leaf-key namespace the hardware flag is inferred to test is *non-empty for Yubico*, not theoretical — https://developers.yubico.com/WebAuthn/Concepts/FIDO_Metadata_Service_(MDS).html
- MDS defines that field as RFC 5280 method 1 (SHA-1 over the `subjectPublicKey` BIT STRING) and states it "implies that the attestation certificate(s) are dedicated to a single authenticator model" — https://fidoalliance.org/specs/mds/fido-metadata-statement-v3.1-ps-20250521.html
- A **complete PEM of a real 2024-12-01-PKI Yubico leaf** is public: `C=SE; O=Yubico AB; OU=Authenticator Attestation; CN=Yubico FIDO EE Serial 140694761812472`, issuer `CN=Yubico FIDO Attestation B 1`, plus the whole B chain and `Yubico Attestation Root 1` — in a GitHub issue body, not an attachment — https://github.com/OpenConext/Stepup-Webauthn/issues/225
- A second real leaf, from the old PKI, is public **with its full EC public key in hex**: `CN=Yubico U2F EE Serial 1573932769`, issuer `Yubico U2F Root CA Serial 457200631`, pubkey `04:23:69:e0:0b:3e:…:80:d4`, and its `id-fido-gen-ce-aaguid` extension reads `2fc0579f811347eab116bb5a8db9202a` — https://github.com/Yubico/java-webauthn-server/issues/66 , independently re-parsed (X/Y split) at https://github.com/psanford/fido-ident
- That settles leaf vs anchor scope: Yubico **leaves carry the model AAGUID extension and are per-model**, while the anchors are vendor-wide — the opposite of the row-27 global-anchor finding, and the reason a leaf is a 1:1 model artifact — https://developers.yubico.com/U2F/Attestation_and_Metadata
- Yubico publishes a batch-certificate *distribution channel* in its own metadata format: `trustedCertificates` is a list of PEM attestation certs where "a certificate in the `trustedCertificates` list is trusted, and its own signature is not regarded (whether it be self-signed or not)" — https://developers.yubico.com/U2F/Attestation_and_Metadata/JSON_Format.html ; the files actually served today (`yubico-u2f-ca-certs.txt`, `PKI/yubico-ca-certs.txt`) contain **CAs only**, including `Yubico Attestation Root 1` (issued 2024-12-01) — https://developers.yubico.com/u2f/yubico-u2f-ca-certs.txt
- The accepted AAGUID set in the brief is incomplete for 5.7-generation 5C NFC silicon: at least `1ac71f64-468d-4fe0-bef1-0e5f2f551f18` (5C NFC 5.7, annotated "EA capable"), `6ab56fad-881f-4a43-acb2-0be065924522` (5C NFC 5.7.4), `fcc0118f-cd45-435b-8da1-9782b2da0715` and `79f3c8ba-9e35-484b-8f47-53a5a0f5c630` (5C NFC FIPS 5.7.4) exist — https://www.powershellgallery.com/packages/entraYK/0.6.0/Content/Private%5CGet-YubiKeyInfo.ps1

## EXPERIMENTS
1. **Key-ID audit of a known-real leaf (highest information, ~15 min).** Save the PEM for `CN=Yubico FIDO EE Serial 140694761812472` from Stepup-Webauthn #225; extract the bare `subjectPublicKey` BIT STRING (`openssl x509 -pubkey -noout` → SPKI DER → drop the 26-byte P-256 `AlgorithmIdentifier` header), SHA-1 it, lowercase-hex it, and grep the decoded current MDS payload for that string; then repeat for every MDS key-identifier list. | expect: either one exact hit (the 5.7 branch *does* register leaf keys) or zero (only 5Ci/U2F-era entries ever did) | proves: whether the flag's presumed input is populated for the 5.7 branch at all — a zero makes batch-leaf replay structurally dead for `f4ce5fc0`/`d7781e5d`, a hit hands us a publicly-available registered SPKI.
2. **Run the same SHA-1 over all public corpus leaves + a control.** Corpus: the `2fc0579f` leaf pubkey hex from java-webauthn-server #66, the #225 Bio leaf, and the `B2 1` CA SPKI as control (row 28 predicts the control misses). Extend `tools/mds-anchor-audit.py` to sweep key identifiers instead of anchors. | expect: 0–1 hits, control = 0 | proves: whether *any* published Yubico EE key is registered anywhere in MDS, i.e. whether a self-issued cert can inherit a real registered SPKI without a private key.
3. **SPKI transplant, AAGUID = `f4ce5fc0` (the actual attempt).** Build a leaf whose `subjectPublicKey` is the #225 real leaf's key, whose `id-fido-gen-ce-aaguid` = `f4ce5fc0`, `CA:FALSE`, and whose `x5c` = [transplanted leaf, `Yubico FIDO Attestation B 1`, `Yubico Attestation Intermediate B 1`, `Yubico Attestation Root 1`]; `fmt: "packed"`, `sig` arbitrary bytes (row 3 unverified). | expect: 200 enrolment either way; flag is the datum | proves: if `true`, key-ID matching is **global across AAGUIDs** and neither signature nor cert provenance matters (this is the whole hypothesis); if `false` with the chain accepted, the key-ID check is per-AAGUID or not key-ID based.
4. **Transplant with the AAGUID extension omitted.** Identical to #3 but `flags = 0x45` with ED asserted and no `id-fido-gen-ce-aaguid`, then a third run carrying a *deliberately wrong* extension value under the same transplanted key. | expect: 200 for all three | proves: whether the verifier's key-ID path consults the extension, and whether row 23's cross-check applies when the SPKI (not the AAGUID) is the matched attribute.
5. **Admission sweep of the newly surfaced 5.7 AAGUIDs with `fmt: "none"`.** `1ac71f64-468d-4fe0-bef1-0e5f2f551f18`, `6ab56fad-881f-4a43-acb2-0be065924522`, `b7d3f68e-88a6-471e-9ecf-2df26d041ede`, `ff4dac45-ede8-4ec2-aced-cf66103f4335`, `79f3c8ba-9e35-484b-8f47-53a5a0f5c630`, `fcc0118f-cd45-435b-8da1-9782b2da0715`. | expect: mixed 200/400; 400 on a sibling AAGUID of the same physical 5C NFC would be decisive | proves: admission is per-AAGUID (allowlist of identifiers), not per-model-per-vendor, and expands the set of enrol-able models beyond the brief's four.
6. **Trust-branch staleness A/B in one session.** Enrol `f4ce5fc0` three times: chained A1 (2024-12), B1 (2024-12), B2 (2025-08). | expect: same flag on all three | proves: whether their trust store is partial (branch choice becomes a lever) or root-set-complete (staleness is not the discriminator) — this is the only remaining handle on the "snapshot vs live blob" unknown.
7. **`qualifying_count` oracle + time decay on every row produced above.** Patch `window.fetch` on the passkeys page to log the `Authorization` header, then `POST /backend-api/cyber_verification/refresh_status` at t+2/10/30 min alongside the per-credential flags via `tools/flag-poller.mjs`. | expect: counts track flags exactly; no late flip from software credentials | proves: whether the counter disagrees with per-credential classification (a separate internal list) and whether re-evaluation is time-driven.
8. **Corpus acquisition, parallel to the above (~30 min).** GitHub code search API for `f4ce5fc0` and `Yubico FIDO EE Serial` in issues/gists, plus a reply to the unanswered Cloudflare poster offering to take up their offer of a 5.7.4 Security Key C NFC sample. | expect: 0–3 additional full-statement blobs | proves: whether a same-model 5.7 leaf exists publicly at all, which is what experiment 1 needs to be conclusive for `f4ce5fc0` rather than for the Bio.

## NEW ANGLES
- **Chase the five registered 5Ci keys, not the unregistered leaves.** The identifiers in Yubico's 5Ci entry (`bf7bcaa0…`, `753300d6…`, `b753a0e4…`, `b6d44a4b…`, `6d491f22…`) are registered by construction, so any EE cert behind them is a *known-matching* SPKI — look for those certs in archived copies of Yubico's legacy U2F metadata JSON (`trustedCertificates`, PEM, signature "not regarded") via web.archive.org, or vendored in old `u2fval` / `python-u2flib-server` releases.
- **Invert the model: enumerate non-empty key-ID lists first, then check admission against them.** If the hardware flag is the key-ID field, then only models whose MDS entry has that field can ever flip it; cross-reference those AAGUIDs against rows 10/13/14/19's refusals — the refused classes may be exactly the key-ID-anchored ones, which would explain the model gate without a hardcoded list.
- **Enterprise Attestation ZIPs as a lawful source of vendor-issued certs.** SecureAuth documents Yubico shipping per-customer attestation certificate files; since `attStmt.sig` is unchecked, a cert file alone is sufficient material — establish whether an EA ZIP leaf is EC, whether its AAGUID is custom, and whether EA leaves are registered in the key-ID field.
- **Model-level vs AAGUID-level diffusion.** The 5C NFC now spans at least six AAGUIDs across 5.7/5.7.4/EA/FIPS; dump all AAGUIDs from `Yubico hardware FIDO2 AAGUIDs` (support.yubico.com/s/article/YubiKey-hardware-FIDO2-AAGUIDs) and probe each — a spread of 200/400 inside one model collapses the "hardware list" theory and replaces it with an identifier allowlist.
- **Diff BLOB 281 vs current for accepted AAGUIDs** to test reclassification over time, and watch whether `ff4dac45`/`b7d3f68e` (post-5.7-generational) ever move, since they are the newest entries and the most likely to sit on the wrong side of a snapshot.
