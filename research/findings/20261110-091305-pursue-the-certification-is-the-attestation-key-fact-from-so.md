A software CTAP2 daemon on macOS cannot be L2 certified. So this angle is self-defeating for the emulator you're driving. Source: https://fidoalliance.org/specs/fido-security-requirements-v1.0-fd-20170524/fido-authenticator-allowed-restricted-operating-environments-list_20170524.html
