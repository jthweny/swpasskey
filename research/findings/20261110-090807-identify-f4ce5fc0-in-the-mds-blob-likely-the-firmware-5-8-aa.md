Experiments 1, 2 and 6 separate them in under an hour; only the second hypothesis makes a certified-software or end-entity-anchored model reachable.
- **`attestationTypes: ["self"]` survey.** Nobody has enumerated MDS entries that declare self attestation *and* `keyProtection` containing hardware/secure_element. If such an entry exists and is admitted, its attestation is reproducible by anyone — that is the direct answer to "any accepted model with attestation material a software authenticator can legitimately hold", and it is a query, not a guess.
- **OpenSK is the only vendor-sanctioned "you hold the attestation key" model** (Google documents replacing `opensk_cert.pem`/`opensk.key` and setting an arbitrary AAGUID). Its MDS anchor is an *end-entity* cert, which is an X.509 shape most verifiers mishandle. Whether `664d9f67` is admitted is one enrolment away; whether the flag survives without the anchor's key is the fork that decides if key possession matters at all.
- **Verifier fingerprinting instead of guessing.** Take the 20 (AAGUID, chain, fmt) inputs you already have, replay them against candidate implementations (python-fido2 `verify_registration_response`, SimpleWebAuthn packed/apple/tpm verifiers, `fido2-lib` with MDS) and see which library reproduces your exact 200/400 pattern; the one that matches tells you which X.509 checks are live (path length, EKU, name constraints, partial chain, anchor-as-leaf).
- **Enterprise attestation as a distinct request path.** CTAP 2.1 enterprise attestation returns a batch cert gated on an RP-ID allowlist burned into the key; if the ChatGPT client requests `attestation: "enterprise"`, the cert shape and possibly the flag logic differ from standard packed. Observe what the client actually asks for before designing more leaves.
- **`tac1` is not identity-gated** (requirements are checked before identity verification), and "Set up your Daybreak keys" is a separate step that takes a chosen qualifying credential. Sequence matters: the cheapest route to signal 4 may be one genuine bundle key plus whatever the emulator is doing, with the two questions (a) flag on the credential and (b) account unlock cleanly decoupled.
- **Vendor-of-record route is priced, not blocked.** MDS listing does not require certification and the submission terms are thin; the real cost is FIDO membership + lab time *if* certification is wanted. Worth pricing before investing more in forgery, because a self-registered AAGUID with a customer-held attestation key is the only durable version of this.
