## FOUND
- NXP EdgeLock SE05x ships pre-provisioned with an NXP attestation keypair, and variants C/E/F additionally contain an NXP-attestation-CA-signed certificate readable by the customer; OEMs may instead generate their own attestation keypair and provision "an attestation certificate (K_ATT_CERT) signed by a trusted CA" — i.e., somebody must sign it, but the factory-signed variant exists per chip out of the box — https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN13254.pdf
- Every EdgeLock SE05x is "trust provisioned with an attestation key and its associated signed certificate" from NXP's own secure injection; the SE050 datasheet confirms "a customer has the possibility to define own attestation keys" and "attestation certificates signed by an attestation CA are included in certain SE050 configurations" — https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/nxp/application-notes/AN12664.pdf , https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/SE050-DATASHEET.pdf
- NXP runs a Trust Provisioning service that performs "customized and secure injection of die-individual keys and credentials into EdgeLock SE05x on behalf" of customers — a provisioning-as-a-service where the customer nominates the credential set — https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/application-note/AN12399.pdf
- NXP also runs a full managed PKI for silicon (EdgeLock 2GO, NXP acting as a CSA-approved Product Attestation Authority, issuing and injecting device credentials into SE05x/A5000/i.MX/K32W) — proof NXP operates commercial attestation-issuance infrastructure, currently scoped to Matter not FIDO — https://www.nxp.com/company/about-nxp/smarter-world-blog/BL-EDGELOCK-2GO
- Infineon OPTIGA TPM has two parallel CA/provisioning services: Sectigo IoT Identity Platform automates certificate provisioning for OPTIGA TPM 2.0 at factory (REST-API CA-as-a-service), and Avnet offers "OPTIGA TPM Mass Pre-Provisioning Service" zero-touch credential injection — in both, a certificate authority signs device keys during manufacture, and the customer CA arrangement is configurable — https://www.sectigo.com/resource-library/sectigo-partners-with-infineon-to-protect-iot-devices , https://www.infineon.com/dgdl/Infineon-ISPN-use-case-sectigo-ApplicationBrochure-v03_00-EN.pdf?fileId=5546d4625b3ca4ec015b423ea5963e59 , https://www.avnet.com/apac/products/product-highlights/infineon-optiga-tpm-mass-pre-provisioning-service
- Token2 PIN+ keys carry a **per-unit unique attestation certificate** issued by Token2's CA (cert embedded in each key and mirrored in MDS), while Token2 Pico keys contain **no attestation certificate at all** ("hardware that is not FIDO certified") and still function as FIDO2 authenticators — Token2's "programmable" line is TOTP-seed only and has no FIDO attestation surface — https://www.token2.com/pages/how-to-verify-your-token2-fido2-key
- Token2 publishes a per-AAGUID MDS introspection tool showing the attestation root of each model; e.g., FT-JCOS FIDO Fingerprint Card anchors to "Feitian FIDO Root CA (Feitian Technologies)" — one Feitian root covers multiple FT-JCOS-based products/brands, consistent with white-label attestation under a shared Feitian anchor — https://www.token2.com/tools/fido2-mds/8c97a7303f7b41a687d61e9b62bda6f0
- Feitian sells blank FT-JCOS JavaCards (Infineon SLE78/SLE77 chips) and states the card "provides customization services" for third-party applets and mass-quantity personalization — a customization/perso channel exists, but no public offer to sign customer leaves under "Feitian FIDO Root CA" — https://www.ftsafe.com/store/product/dual-interface-java-card-smart-card-with-cc-eal5
- A vendor (VivoKey/Dangerous Things, staff account) states directly that an official attestation certificate "can only be deployed to the Apex… as it requires a secure delivery channel to not leak out", and that shipping an attestation cert for customer use "would not fly with the security / non-forgery requirements of FIDO" — the clearest sourced statement that vendors will not sign customer leaves or release attestation keys — https://forum.dangerousthings.com/t/vivokey-attestation-approval-changed/26307
- Same thread: an MDS entry **without FIDO certification is possible** — the VivoKey Apex was "only listed in the FIDO metabase as uncertified" — but "updating a MDS entry is hard and sometimes impossible"; the practical certification cost is "$10,000+ and months", including mandatory in-person interoperability testing at events held "a couple times a year" — https://forum.dangerousthings.com/t/vivokey-attestation-approval-changed/26307
- Same thread + Microsoft doc: Microsoft removed MDS-listed-but-**uncertified** models from Entra's attestation-eligible list (~Aug 2025) and now enforces certification at *any* level plus MDS presence — an RP classification list demonstrably keyed on certification status, changed unilaterally, matching the TrustKey flip-flop pattern — https://forum.dangerousthings.com/t/vivokey-attestation-approval-changed/26307 , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/authentication/concept-fido2-hardware-vendor
- Dangerous Things **flexSecure** is a genuine-secure-element platform where the customer "deploy[s] the FIDO2 applet yourself, and also generate, sign and deploy your own attestation certificate" — a purchasable device whose attestation key is customer-generated by design (the class the brief flags as a live exception) — https://forum.dangerousthings.com/t/vivokey-attestation-approval-changed/26307
- SoloKeys: standard Solo 2 ships with locked firmware and "SoloKeys attestation certificates"; **Solo 2 Hacker ships with no attestation certificates at all** ("It does not include attestation certificates") — and for Solo 1 the hacker-edition attestation key is described as "basically public knowledge" — candidate for the sourced leaked-key exception — https://solokeys.com/pages/solo-2-guide , https://solokeys.com/policies/terms-of-service , https://github.com/solokeys/kickstarter2021/discussions/75
- Microsoft's vendor contract makes explicit what the chain half of the gate needs: "Vendors are responsible for publishing all root attestation certificates to the FIDO Alliance MDS; otherwise, attestation verification can fail", and enforced attestation requires "a complete certificate that chains back to the attestation roots extracted from the FIDO Alliance MDS" — https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/authentication/concept-fido2-hardware-vendor
